Bootstrapping and Deploying Live to Your Cloud Environment
==========================================================
The Bootstrap Script: This sets up a secure, management state storage tier inside your AWS account to keep tracking metrics logs for resource changes.

cdk bootstrap aws://YOUR_AWS_ACCOUNT_ID/us-east-1

The Deploy Trigger: This compiles your Python objects into an explicit CloudFormation deployment engine file and safely injects your live pipeline assets into your cloud instance.

cdk deploy
